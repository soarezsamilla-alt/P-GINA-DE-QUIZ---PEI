# **App Name**: PEI Navigator

## Core Features:

- Interactive Quiz Progression: Guides users through a multi-screen questionnaire to identify their needs regarding PEI models, building engagement for the product.
- Personalized Product Presentation Tool: Utilizes AI to analyze quiz responses and dynamically generate tailored descriptive text for the product overview, emphasizing benefits most relevant to the user's expressed needs.
- Comprehensive Product Showcase: Displays detailed information about the PEI models, including the number of templates (47), formats (PDF, editable Word), page count (48), and target grades (Fundamental I and II).
- Visual Sample Gallery: Presents 2-3 marked 'Amostra' (sample) screenshots of the PEI models to provide a tangible preview of the product quality.
- Customer Testimonials Display: Features authentic social proof, such as screenshots of satisfied customer reviews from WhatsApp, to build trust and credibility.
- Prominent Offer & Call-to-Action: Clearly presents the special offer (50% Off - R$ 18,90) and a direct call-to-action button ('ACESSAR AGORA!') to encourage immediate purchase.
- Secure Payment Gateway Redirection: Upon clicking the purchase button, securely redirects the user to the designated external payment platform for transaction completion.

## Style Guidelines:

- The overall aesthetic adopts a light color scheme to convey clarity and professionalism, suitable for an educational context.
- Primary color: A deep, calming blue (#295C99). This hue evokes trust, stability, and professionalism, reflecting the reliability of educational materials.
- Background color: A very light, desaturated blue (#EEF3F7), almost white, providing a clean and non-distracting canvas that ensures excellent readability for all content.
- Accent color: A vibrant yet professional blue-violet (#4533DB). This contrasting color is used to highlight key interactive elements and calls to action, drawing the user's eye without being overwhelming.
- Headline and body text font: 'Inter' (sans-serif). Chosen for its high readability across various screen sizes, modern aesthetic, and neutral, objective feel, ensuring clear communication of educational content.
- Utilize clear, professional vector icons that symbolize education, learning, success, and digital documents (e.g., checkmarks, graduation caps, pencils, document icons) to enhance navigation and comprehension.
- Implement a clean, mobile-first, single-column layout for the quiz and product presentation, focusing on straightforward user progression. Content blocks will be clearly segmented with adequate whitespace.
- Incorporate subtle and smooth transition animations between quiz screens and hover effects on interactive elements like option selections and buttons to create a responsive and engaging user experience without distraction.
